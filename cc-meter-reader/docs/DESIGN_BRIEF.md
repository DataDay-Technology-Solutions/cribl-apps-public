# Meter Reader — Design Brief (binding for every screen)

This brief replaces the missing `METER_READER_DESIGN.md` (DECISIONS D2). PRD §8 and §8.8 ("Definition of Beautiful") are the source; this file makes them concrete so parallel builders ship one coherent product. The bar: **it looks better than what Cribl ships, on a projector, at a glance, in both themes.** Capra details are in `docs/platform/capra.md`; use only real exports.

## 1. Identity: "Reads the meter. Sends the receipt."
Two motifs, used sparingly and never as decoration for its own sake:
- **The meter**: one huge, calm, ticking number (the hero). Tabular numerals, digits roll, nothing jitters.
- **The receipt**: itemized money lines with **dot leaders** in `Source Code Pro` (Capra's mono face): `Windows event trimming ........ $1,340 / day`. Used for Top savers, Show the math line items, the weekly receipt and the Slack preview. The hero card on the Receipt view has a subtle **perforated bottom edge** (a 6 px zig-zag mask on the card's bottom border), the only skeuomorphic touch in the app.

## 3. Type scale (one system)
- **Display (hero money)**: Open Sans 600, `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`, `letter-spacing: -0.02em`. Sizes: 88 px desktop, 56 px at 390 px, **≥ 160 px presenter** at 1920. `clamp()` between them.
- **Title**: `typography.heading.lg` (24 px) once per page; card titles `typography.heading.sm` (16 px).
- **Body**: Capra body (14 px). **Caption**: 12–13 px `color.foreground.subtle`.
- **Money figures anywhere**: tabular-nums; whole dollars; thousands separators; units as a secondary-text suffix (`/ day`, `/ year`), never inside the big number. Percentages are integers. Bytes: `12.3 GB`.
- `text-wrap: balance` on headlines; no orphans in captions; SVG roots set `font-family` explicitly (no default serif anywhere).

## 4. Motion budget
- Meter: rAF-driven at `ratePerSecM`; digits roll; bounding box fixed (reserve width with tabular nums + a fixed digit count per magnitude); eases to the authoritative value over 900 ms on each snapshot; never visibly goes backwards (SPEC 13 Ticking).
- Ribbons ease to a new snapshot over 1.2 s ease-out, so a ribbon is seen to narrow and its wedge to grow (a re-fit of the same figures 200 ms; the What-if morph 400 ms); the in-ribbon drift (≥ 8 s per cycle) runs on the highlighted path only, paused off screen; both stop under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Incident takeover** is the ONLY thing that moves onto the screen by itself: 450 ms ease-out slide-up (reduced motion → 200 ms fade).
- Nothing pulses, bounces or loops for attention. Skeleton shimmer ≤ 1.2 s then static.

## 5. Screens (desktop 1440 layout; 12-column grid, 16 px gutters, max content width 1280, centered)
### 5.1 Receipt (`/`, default landing)
1. **Hero card** (12 col): label `Saved by Cribl` (heading.sm, subtle) + period toggle `MTD · Today · 30 days · Annualized · Custom` (a segmented button group; **Custom** opens a Capra Popover under the group's left edge, and pressing Custom again re-opens it — quick picks `1 h · 6 h · 24 h · 7 days · 30 days` as a second segmented group, an exact `From` / `To` window in the display timezone (one field per row, never empty: they open showing the window Apply would use, a quick pick fills them with its own), a one-line preview of the window that will be summed ("Summed in whole hours as Sep 19, 2:00 PM–Sep 26, 2:00 PM (7 days)."), `Cancel` / `Apply`; Escape closes and focus returns to the toggle; disabled on sample data, with a one-line hint under the label row) on the same row; the **hero number** (`data-callout="saved"`); with a range applied, the window in words on its own line (`Sep 26, 10:00 AM–2:00 PM (4 h)`, body.md semibold) and a static number — a closed window never ticks and is never annualized; caption beneath: period basis ("month to date · collecting since 9:41 PM" / "annualized run rate, from the last 5 days"; for a range its exactness, "minute-exact" / "whole hours · 1,380 of 1,440 minutes metered" / "whole UTC days · 6 of 7 days metered", "widened to whole hours" when an exact window was rounded outward and "through the last whole hour" when it stops at the last completed bucket, then "collecting since …" when clipped, then `≈ $X a day at this rate` — on a phone the rate takes its own line); while the range's rows are read the number, caption and bar are static ghost blocks of the finished layout's height (visible in both themes; the words show throughout, nothing moves when the figures land), a failed read is an inline notice with Retry under the words, no wider than a sentence; then the **receipt bar**: one full-width horizontal bar whose total length is would-have-paid (`data-callout="whp"`), the grey segment is paid (`data-callout="paid"`), the green segment is saved; labels under it: `You would have paid $68,412 · You paid $27,181 · 60% saved`. Optional smaller line: `Net after Cribl $33,230 · Paid for itself 4.2×`. Right-aligned in the header row: `Show the math` (link-button) and `Copy receipt`. Perforated bottom edge. "How this number is made ▸" disclosure under the bar expands the 4-step strip.
2. Row: **Saved over the last 30 days** (8 col; SVG area chart of daily saved, faint deploy-marker diamonds on the x-axis, hover tooltip) + **What's saving the most** (4 col; top 5 receipt lines with dot leaders, each a deep link to the pipeline).
3. Row: **Where the money goes** (8 col; one horizontal stacked bar per destination: paid grey + saved green, $/day at the end, counterfactual + unpriced chips) + **Alerts** (4 col; open incidents as compact incident cards; "No open alerts" empty state).
4. Unpriced notice (inline, above row 2) when any: "3 destinations are unpriced. Set prices to include them." with link.
- 390 px: single column in the same order; hero 56 px; the receipt bar stays.

### 5.2 Presenter (`?present=1`, key `P`)
Dark by default on stage. Chrome hidden. ≥ 64 px margins; nothing within 40 px of an edge. Hero ≥ 160 px centered-left with a single soft radial glow (the only gradient in the app) in `--mr-saved` at ~12% opacity; caption ≥ 28 px ("annualized run rate, from the last N days"); under it, on live data only, "Saved since you started watching $12.40 · ~$0.04 a second", from $0.00 when the stage opens and accruing at the measured rate, frozen grey when the data is not live — with the takeover's lost counter, **the only money shown with cents**; top-five list ≥ 32 px with dot leaders on the right half; **QR ≥ 220 px** bottom-right (`data-callout="qr"`) with a caption that says where the code goes (D51): "Meter Reader · get the App on GitHub" for the default (the App's home in the public repository), "Meter Reader · join the Cribl Innovators Network" for the demo org's group, else "Meter Reader · <host>" — nothing the code does not lead to; scene indicator bottom-left (demo build). **Incident takeover**: full-width card over the lower half: severity glyph + title; before → after ratio as two large numbers with an arrow; `$25 a day · $9,125 a year` (`data-callout="per-day"`); commit line `a1f3c9e "demo: break the trim on mrd_pay_sample" · s.koelpin` (`data-callout="commit"`, `data-callout="author"` on the username); under the ratio a live `Lost since the deploy $0.83` counter in the incident red (cents, the incident's $/day from the deploy on; frozen on the recovery card as `Cost $1.23 before it recovered` in the default ink); live `Caught in 2:51` clock that flips to `Sent to Slack ✓ 11:44:03` when delivery lands; "1-minute confirmation (demo profile). Default is 3." when applicable. Stays until any key or 45 s. Recovery variant: green, 10 s, "Recovered · savings back to 75% · closed itself."

### 5.3 Flow (`/flow`)
Subtitle always visible: "Cribl Insights shows this map in bytes. This is dollars." d3-sankey Source → Pipeline → Destination, node padding ≥ 12 px, band width = sqrt of the flow's would-have-paid $/day (dollars, not bytes; the byte map is an optional mode), ribbons and source nodes coloured by their **Source** (D55: a calm Cribl-toned set, the five sources with the most would-have-paid each get one of five measured hue families, chosen by the source's id so a colour never follows rank and is the same on the map, the byte map, the What-if, the stage, the Story and the phone list; a source past five is one neutral, named by a sentence in the map's note, never a repeated hue; destination and pipeline nodes are ink, so no source shares its destination's colour), **saved wedge** = hatched `--mr-saved-tint` region cut into the same ribbon where the pipeline narrows it (its share of the band = saved ÷ would-have-paid); $/day label on a subtle backing plate on every ribbon worth ≥ 2 % of the map's would-have-paid; labels outside bands, collision-avoided or truncated with tooltip; hover/focus highlights a full path and shows ONE receipt card (In / Out / Would have paid / Paid / Saved / Paying now $/hour; the last line and the hover say "Paying now", D48). Keyboard focusable nodes with a styled focus ring. Empty state: "Set a price to see dollars on this map".

### 5.4 Ledger (`/ledger`)
Toolbar (search, filters: state, destination, group) → virtualized table (sticky header, right-aligned tabular numbers, row hover, 60-min sparkline as a single stroke, incident chips) → **Change timeline** (savings ratio over 24 h / 7 d with commit diamonds; click → hover card with message, user, what moved) → **Incidents rail** (right side on desktop, below on mobile) with delivery status. Deep link `?object=` scrolls to and highlights the row.

### 5.5 Settings (`/settings/*`)

### 5.9 What-if (dry run) — `/whatif` and a toggle on the Flow view (Steve, 9/26 00:30)
"If we apply this pack, here is what happens — then flip it on." A projection mode that never touches config by itself.
- **Pick**: a stream (route) + a treatment: one of the packs (Windows XML, Palo Alto + syslog, VPC Flow aggregation), "Go aggressive", or a custom "drop N % of bytes" slider.
- **Estimate, with a named basis** (shown in the math): (1) *measured by dry run* — the treatment's pipeline run against real sample events of that source through the Leader's preview API, bytes in vs out; else (2) *measured on a similar stream* — the live ratio of a stream in this workspace already running that treatment (e.g. `windows-dc` runs the Windows XML pack at 33 %); else (3) *documented range* — the pack README's range, shown as a range.
- **Show**: the Flow map morphs to a ghosted "after" state (dashed outline for projected ribbons, the saved wedge in hatched green, a PROJECTION chip), a before → after comparison strip (`$ / day`, `$ / year`, ratio, GB/day), and the Receipt hero preview ("Saved by Cribl would read $140,210 annualized, +$44,580").
- **Flip it on** (demo build only): "Apply for real" runs the real lever (commit + deploy, confirmation first), then the view switches to *projected vs actual* and fills in the measured number as sweeps arrive ("projected 33 %, measured 34 % after 3 min").
- Release build: the calculator only (no Apply button), which is still the question every platform owner asks: "what would this pack save me?"
- Motion: the morph is user-initiated (toggle), 400 ms, reduced-motion → crossfade. Colour rules unchanged; the projection is dashed/hatched, never a new hue.

### 5.10 Report card — `/report` (from the Receipt hero's **Report card** button)
"The savings on one page for leadership." A view and the document it produces; the document is paper in both themes.
- **View** (Page title "Report card", a "Back to the receipt" link): controls card (4 col) beside the preview (8 col); 390 px one column, controls first. Controls: period toggle `Month to date · Today · 30 days` (+ `Custom range` when the Receipt's `?range` is active on live data; carried from the Receipt via the sticky params), `Prepared for` (TextField) and `Note` (TextArea), both never saved, then "Before you send it" (what the sender can act on and the document's reader can't: typical list prices rather than contract rates, no Cribl cost set, destinations with no price; never printed on the document), then one primary `Download PDF` and secondary `Download HTML`, `Copy for email`, `Download CSV`; the sample note under them in sample mode. Preview: the downloadable HTML itself in `<iframe sandbox="" srcdoc>`, framed like a card, caption "The preview is the file you download." The view pins the snapshot it opened with (a document never reloads under its reader); when a sweep lands, a one-line notice above the preview offers `Update figures`. Before the first snapshot the same layout with the actions disabled and one sentence saying why.
- **Document** (core/report-pdf.ts, report-html.ts, report-email.ts): a 5 px green band; `METER READER` eyebrow; title "Cribl savings report card" with the period right-aligned; the byline (prepared for · prepared by · generated). Hero: "Saved by Cribl, <period>" eyebrow, the figure in money green at display size, the receipt bar (grey paid, green saved, the whole bar would-have-paid), "You would have paid · You paid · N% of dollars saved", and one line saying what the prices are ("At typical list prices for 8 destinations, not contract rates"). Printed money adds up: would have paid − paid = saved in whole dollars, and a totals row is the sum of the rows above it (where a destination is paid for but saves nothing, a line under the totals says so). Four KPI tiles, all as tall as the fullest (annual run rate; Cribl paid for itself N× with "Every $1 of Cribl saved $X", "ROI N% (net ÷ cost)" and the net; data volume "N% less data", what would have reached your destinations → what did; protection, with "in the last 24 hours only" when the alert history is shorter than the period, "at risk until fixed" only for an open alert, and durations in words, never a clock time), the daily trend as bars on the Receipt chart's round ticks down to $0 (today lighter, "so far"), the top savers with volume reduced beside saved per day, then provenance right after them. One annual figure only: the run rate; the per-day tables say "at the last hour's rate (× 24)" and a line under the destinations points to the run rate for planning. Detail: where the money goes (priced as <preset>, "<preset>, your rate" or "Your rate"; $/GB; per day in → out; would have paid / paid / saved; totals; a destination priced elsewhere names that price), protection cards (open: per day until fixed and a year if left; recovered: how long it lasted), how these numbers are made, prices used (typical range, basis, the publisher's name linked to the source). Nothing is cut to fit: names, flows, notes and sources wrap; a heading keeps its first rows and a last row never goes to a page alone. On a phone every table row becomes a card with Saved first and the other amounts as labelled lines. Dollars and volume are always two labelled percentages. Sample data: a band on page 1, a faint diagonal "SAMPLE DATA" watermark and a tag in every footer. Colours are the light tokens (green-9 fills, green-11 text, slate-9 paid); type is Helvetica in the PDF and the system sans in HTML and email.

## 6. Every state is designed
Loading (skeleton matching final layout), empty, first-run, unpriced, rate-limited ("Rate limited by the Leader. Next sweep in 0:42."), host-not-authorized, 403 (inline per section, actions disabled), error (toast once + "last updated" caption), replay, sample band, muted chip ("muted after demo reset · 6 min"), demo-profile notice. A state without a design is a bug.

## 8. Story/video callout ids (must exist on the elements)
`whp`, `paid`, `saved`, `change-marker`, `commit`, `author`, `per-day`, `slack-message`, `receipt-total`, `qr` — as `data-callout` attributes.
- The Story's dollar-map beat (P2-W11) adds two on the Flow map's own SVG: **`flow-plate`**, a ribbon's $ / day plate ("priced at the destination"), and **`flow-saved`**, the $-saved plate on that ribbon's hatched wedge ("what the pipeline removed").
